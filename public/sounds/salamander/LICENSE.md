# The Salamander piano

`acoustic_grand_piano-mp3.json` in this folder is the Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, a Yamaha C5, converted for the web.
It is under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0, CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) license.

- **Attribution:** Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, <https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html>
- **Changes:** 30 notes of one velocity layer, each trimmed to its onset, shortened with a fade, set to one gain and encoded as MP3.

`tools/import-sounds.ts` makes it from <https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/SalamanderGrandPiano/SalamanderGrandPianoV3+20161209_44khz16bit.tar.xz>.
