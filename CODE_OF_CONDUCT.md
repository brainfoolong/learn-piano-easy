# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its full text applies to every space of the project, such as its issues and pull requests.

## In short

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are learning, both piano and code.
- Criticise ideas, never people.
- Harassment, insults and unwelcome attention are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer by e-mail, at the address on the site's
[legal notice](https://learn-piano-easy.app/imprint/). Every report is read and kept confidential. The
maintainer acts as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
