# 0011: Every piano the site draws is the 3D piano

## Decision

- The site draws one piano: the 3D piano of `src/render/piano.ts`. The 2D SVG keyboard is deleted.
- Lessons, the setup check and the gallery show it through `pianoKeys()` of `src/render/piano-keys.ts`.
  That is the piano alone, framed close, without notes or lanes.
- A lesson makes one view and hands it to each step, so it holds one WebGL context.
- The landing page's devices band shows a picture until a click, as decision 0008 says for every landing
  piano.

This extends decision 0008 from the notes to the piano itself.

## Why

A learner saw the 3D piano in the player and in a lesson's songs, and a flat one in every other step.
Two pianos had to be kept in step by hand.

The service worker precaches every code chunk. After the first visit the 3D piano loads from the cache,
even offline, so its weight costs a lesson nothing. Only the landing page's first visit pays for it, and
there a click starts it.

## Consequences

- A lesson needs WebGL. Without it the view says so, and the step still hears MIDI and the computer
  keyboard.
- Opening a lesson parses the piano's chunk and starts a WebGL context, which a still step then leaves
  idle.
- The end-to-end tests find a key through the view's key map, an `aria-hidden` layer of one span per key.

## Rejected

- **Keeping the 2D keyboard for small steps:** two pianos would remain, which is what this decision removes.
- **The player's whole scene in each step:** its lanes and notes take room a step needs, and it would
  need a settings store per step.
- **Loading the landing band's piano when it scrolls into view:** every visitor who scrolls would pay
  for it, which decision 0008 rejected for the same reason.
