# 0012: English leads, German follows every change, and the other languages catch up in translation passes

## Decision

- English is the source of every text. Every other locale translates the English.
- A session that adds or changes a text writes it in English and German, and in no other locale.
- Every other locale may lack a text. The site then shows the English text in its place.
- A translation pass brings one locale up to date. It is its own goal or session, never a side task of a
  feature.
- A pass follows [docs/agent/translation.md](../agent/translation.md), which is the home of the method.
- A text whose meaning changes gets a new key or id, so no translation of the old meaning stays in place.
  A text that is only reworded keeps its key, and the next pass reviews its translations.
- The site serves English, German, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, Portuguese, Indonesian, Bengali,
  Russian, French, Urdu and Japanese, as each one's goal is reached.

This extends decision 0004, whose catalogs were all complete.

## Why

The thirteen locales reach about two thirds of the world's people in a language they read well.
Thirteen translations of every change would make each feature many times slower to build. A feature
would also wait on the languages its author cannot read.

A translation done in one pass per locale is better than one done in passing. The pass loads the
locale's style guide and glossary once, and it reviews every text with the same terms and voice.

The author of this project reads English and German, so those two stay complete and are checked by eye.

## Consequences

- A learner of a passed locale may see an English text between two passes.
- The type checker refuses a key that English lacks, so a renamed key drops its old translations.
- A per-locale record of the English each translation was made from tells a pass which texts went stale.
- The check that no English shows on a page runs only on the complete locales: English and German.

## Rejected

- **Every locale complete in every change:** each feature would carry eleven translations nobody on
  the project can read.
- **A missing text shown as its key or left blank:** a learner would read nothing, where English is
  readable for most of them.
- **A stale translation hidden until its pass:** a fixed typo in English would turn eleven locales'
  texts back into English.
