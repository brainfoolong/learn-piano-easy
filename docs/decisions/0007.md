# 0007: The hosted site counts its page views with our own Matomo, without cookies

## Decision

- Each page the site shows sends one request to our own Matomo at `stats.learn-piano-easy.app`.
- The request carries the page's address without its query, its title, and the first page's referrer.
- The site loads no Matomo script and sets no cookie.
- Only a page on `learn-piano-easy.app` sends it. A local build, a test run and a copy of the site send nothing.
- Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control and the privacy policy's switch each stop every request.
- The site publishes a legal notice at `imprint/` and a privacy policy at `privacy/`, in every locale.
- `src/site/stats.ts` owns how a visit is counted.

## Why

The site goes public, and its maker wants to know which pages people use. Austrian law asks a public
site for a legal notice and a privacy policy.

The setup copies the maker's other site, novis-lang.org. One server, one privacy text and one way of
counting then serve both sites.

Without cookies and with a shortened IP address, the count needs no consent banner. It rests on
legitimate interest, and the visitor can object on the privacy page.

## Consequences

- The request is the one network call after the first load. Nothing waits on it, and offline it fails silently.
- The landing page promises no ads and no profiles. It no longer claims that nobody counts.
- The privacy policy changes with any new data that leaves the device.

## Rejected

- **Matomo's own script:** it costs a download on every first load, and it sets cookies unless told otherwise.
- **Counting every host:** local builds and test runs would pollute the numbers.
- **No statistics:** the maker would have no view of which pages people use.
